# AI PC Quest · Intel Core Ultra

An interactive in-store game built for the **Intel 1 Million Minds** challenge (Round 1).

**Play it here:** https://syed-sameena-19.github.io/Intel-AI-PC-Quest/

## The problem
Gen Z shoppers use AI every day, but in Large Format Retail stores like Croma, Reliance Digital and Vijay Sales they often choose a laptop by colour and price, not by what will keep performing for years.

## The idea
A 2–3 minute game that lets a first-time shopper discover why an Intel Core Ultra AI PC is worth choosing, then ends with a personal recommendation and a next step.

## How it plays
- **Quick primer:** CPU, GPU and NPU explained in plain words.
- **Level 1, Engine Rush:** send each task to the right engine (CPU, GPU or NPU) before time runs out.
- **Level 2, Privacy Defender:** keep your files on the device instead of leaking to the cloud.
- **Level 3, Battery Lab:** turn on AI features and compare an older laptop with an Intel Core Ultra AI PC (illustrative model, not a benchmark).
- **Level 4, Store Trap:** scan three laptops and pick one by real specs, not colour or price.
- **Final pick:** choose what matters most (battery, performance, privacy or value).
- **Results:** a personalised recommendation, a persona-based "Why Intel" card, a rival comparison (AMD Ryzen AI, Snapdragon X, older laptops) and an in-store checklist.

## Extras
- Hinglish toggle for walk-in shoppers.
- 30-second quick mode.
- Shareable score card.
- Auto-reset after 90 seconds of no touch, for in-store kiosks.

## Notes on accuracy
Battery and performance figures in the game are illustrative. Real results vary by model, display and workload. Shoppers should always check the exact model's NPU (40+ TOPS for Copilot+ PC features), RAM, battery tests and supported apps.

## Tech
A single self-contained `index.html` file (HTML, CSS and JavaScript). No installs, no backend, and no data collected.
